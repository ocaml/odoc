\section{Module \ocamlinlinecode{Oxcaml\_\allowbreak{}functor\_\allowbreak{}alias}}\label{Oxcaml_functor_alias}%
A functor whose result aliases its argument, applied by \ocamlinlinecode{include functor}.

OxCaml 5.4 rejects such an alias, as OCaml does, so this is kept apart from the \ocamlinlinecode{oxcaml.\allowbreak{}mli} case and runs on earlier versions of OxCaml only.
